Workout Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026 · Developer: Ethan Winiger

The short version

  • Workout Tracker has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics.
  • I don't run any servers. Your training data lives on your device and in your own iCloud.
  • A few optional features send limited information to Apple's iCloud or to other services. Each one is listed below, so you can decide whether to use it.

Your training data

Your workouts, sessions, sets, personal records, notes, schedules and exercise pictures are stored on your device and synced between your own devices through your private iCloud (Apple's CloudKit). That data sits in your iCloud account. I cannot see it. Your app settings (rest timer, units, sounds and voice) stay on each device.

Things you delete wait in Trash inside the app for 30 days and are then removed for good. Delete All Data and Start Over (Profile → Trash) removes everything. You can also remove the app's iCloud data in the iOS Settings app under your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Your username and sharing (optional)

If you set a username or use Share, the app stores these in iCloud's public database: your display name, a share code, an anonymous identifier for your iCloud account (not your name or Apple ID), the workouts you choose to publish together with their pictures, and who follows whom. Anyone who has your share code can see the workouts you publish. You can get a new code at any time.

The public catalog

The catalog of exercises, equipment, muscles, workouts and templates is published by the developer. When you download from it, nothing about you or your training is sent.

The Supporter page (optional)

If you use the Supporter page, the following is stored in iCloud's public database and is visible to other people who use the app and are signed in to iCloud (it is not on the open web):

  • the proposals you write, with up to five photos you attach (resized before upload);
  • your comments and upvotes;
  • your username and an anonymous identifier for your iCloud account.

You can delete your own comments in the app. To have a proposal removed, contact me. Anyone can report a proposal or comment; a report contains a copy of the reported text and is seen only by the developer, who can remove content and remove people from the page. When you block someone, that person is hidden on your device and the developer is told. Your list of blocked people is stored only on your device.

Subscriptions

Supporter subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. I never receive your payment details, Apple ID or personal information. The app asks Apple, on your device, whether you have an active subscription.

Other services that receive data when you use certain features

  • AI exercise pictures (only in versions where the feature is enabled): if you generate a picture, the text description you enter is sent to Google (Gemini) and/or Hugging Face to create the image. No account details are sent. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Exercise videos: videos play through YouTube's player. YouTube (Google) receives your IP address and the video request, as it would in any app or website that shows a YouTube video, and applies its own privacy policy.

What the app does not do

  • It does not show ads, track you across apps or websites, or sell or share your data for advertising.
  • It contains no analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone. Spoken announcements are produced on your device. Photos are only read when you pick them with the system photo picker.

Children

Workout Tracker is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Use only the features you want; sharing and the Supporter page are optional.
  • Delete your data in the app or in iCloud settings, as described above.
  • Ask me to remove something you posted publicly by using the contact address below.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and is stored by Apple in iCloud, which applies its own security measures. No system is perfectly secure, but the app avoids keeping your data anywhere else.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update the date at the top of this page.
