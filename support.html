Workout Tracker — Support

Help, questions and reports

Contact

Email workouttracker.contactinfo@gmail.com. I read every message and aim to reply within two days.

Report a proposal or comment, or someone's behaviour

On the Supporter page, open the ••• menu on a proposal or comment and choose Report, or choose Block to stop seeing that person. Reports go straight to the developer, who removes content that breaks the community rules and can remove people from the page. You can also email me.

Restore a purchase

Profile → the "Help shape the app" card → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Manage or cancel a subscription

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Workout Tracker. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the paid period ends.

My workouts aren't syncing

Check that you are signed in to iCloud on each device (Settings → your name) and that iCloud is on for Workout Tracker. Syncing needs an internet connection and can take a minute.

Delete my data

In the app: Profile → Trash → Delete All Data and Start Over. To remove the app's data from iCloud: Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Workout Tracker. To have something you posted on the Supporter page removed, email me.
